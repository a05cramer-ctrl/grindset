How it works
  1. Click a tree, rock or fishing spot. Your character works it for you and you gain XP. Every skill goes from level 1 to 99.
  2. Full inventory? Sell to the Shopkeeper for coins, then buy a better axe, pickaxe or harpoon from Smith Brunn.
  3. Rare drops are worth real $GRINDSET. Nests, gems, outfits, pets and party hats trade on the Grind Exchange.
  4. The Goblin King storms the pit up north every few minutes. Everyone fights him together for loot.
